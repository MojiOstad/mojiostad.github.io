A little more about Moji.

I’m a developer and experience designer with a foundation in games and Unity. At Wintor, I help creators publish stories and visitors explore them through AR, mobile apps, and browser-based Online Guides.

Since early 2026, my role has expanded into backend and web development, production analytics and support, and infrastructure. I’m continuing to deepen my knowledge of Azure, AI and VR.

Alongside my work with Wintor and Shosho, I’m exploring AI-assisted team workflows and local AI for future museum experiences. That museum AI work is ongoing development.
