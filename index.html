I’m Moji, a developer and experience designer who likes understanding both how things work and how people use them.

My background is in games and Unity. At Wintor, that grew into creating augmented reality experiences—and, from early 2026, developing the web and backend systems, analytics, and production support behind the platform.

I care about the whole journey: helping a creator get their story out, making an interaction feel natural, and keeping the experience available when a visitor arrives.
