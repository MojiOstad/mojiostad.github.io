Marinemuseum · Den Helder

Digital stories, anchored in place.

Location-based museum experiences that connect digital content with the place around the visitor.

My role
Location-based experience work through Wintor
Period
Selected museum work

The context

At Marinemuseum in Den Helder, my work formed part of the location-based experiences created through Wintor.

My contribution

I contributed experience work using Wintor, bringing my background in Unity development and experience design to a museum setting.

The approach

The focus was the relationship between content, place, and visitor interaction: using the platform to make digital stories part of exploring a physical location.

The outcome

This project brings together my experience in interactive storytelling and my work with Wintor: connecting digital content to a physical museum visit.
