Amsterdam in Motion

Designing for visitors.

Turning the stories of a city into interactions people can understand, explore, and take part in.

My role
Experience design and AR interaction refinement through Wintor
Period
2026

The context

Museum experiences ask people to navigate both a physical space and a digital layer. An interaction needs to make sense in the moment, with clear cues about what to do next.

My contribution

I worked on experience design and refinement for Amsterdam in Motion through Wintor. Project work included clearer interaction cues, visitor guidance, subtitles, and audio improvements.

The approach

I refined the details that connect intention to action: highlighting interactive objects, making clickable elements easier to recognize, clarifying placement and instructions, and improving how media supports the story.

The outcome

Completed refinements gave the experience clearer prompts and more accessible media. The work reflects my approach to experience design: observe how an interaction is understood, then improve the cues and feedback around it.
