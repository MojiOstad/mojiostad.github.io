Wintor

Experiences to platform.

Building the visitor experience—and supporting the systems that make it possible.

My role
Unity development, experience design, web & backend development, production analytics and support
Period
2022–present · expanded platform focus from early 2026

The context

Wintor helps creators build and share location-based experiences. Visitors encounter those stories through Android and iOS apps, while Online Guides bring content directly to the browser.

My contribution

I started at Wintor as a Unity developer and experience designer. From early 2026, my work expanded toward backend and web development, live production analytics, and support. I develop features for creators and help maintain the infrastructure behind publishing and content delivery.

The approach

I work across the creator and visitor journey: extending tools, investigating production behavior, and supporting the systems that serve content. My experience designing interactions helps connect technical decisions to what creators and visitors need.

The outcome

My work contributes to Wintor as a live platform used by clients to create and deliver experiences. Android and iOS support and browser-based Online Guides give different audiences ways to access content. I continue developing my knowledge of Azure, AI, and VR as this work grows.
